# Mouthpiece glossary

The names players and refacers use for the parts of a mouthpiece, and the settings in the app that
change each one. Settings are given by their name in the app; [PARAMETERS.md](PARAMETERS.md) has
every setting with pictures. Besides the sliders, **Shape charts** (under the view) show the facing,
the outside and the inside, and **Edit shape** lets you drag their lines.

## The reed side

| Part | What it is | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Table** (reed seat) | The flat where the reed's back lies and the ligature holds it. | **Table length**, **Table width at the tip**, **Table width at the back**, **Table hollow** |
| **Window** | The opening in the table under the reed, between the rails. | **Window length**, **Window width**, **Window narrows at the back**, **Window corner rounding** |
| **Side rails** | The two edges along the window that the reed seals on. | **Side rail width** |
| **Tip rail** (front rail) | The edge across the front that the reed closes against. | **Tip rail thickness**, **Tip roundness** |
| **Facing** (lay) | The rails' gentle curve away from the reed, from the break point to the tip. Both rails need the same curve. | **Facing curve**, **Where the curve opens**, or drag the points on the **Facing curve** chart |
| **Break point** | Where the rails leave the flat table: the start of the facing. | **Facing length** sets how far it is from the tip |
| **Facing length** | From the break point to the tip, in mm. | **Facing length** |
| **Tip opening** | The gap between the reed and the tip rail, measured with a straightedge on the table. Often given in thousandths of an inch. | **Tip opening** |
| **Chamfer** | A small bevel on the inside edge of the rails, around the window. | none: finish it by hand if you want one |

## Inside: the air path from tip to neck

| Part | What it is | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Baffle** | The roof over the reed, just behind the tip rail. Common shapes: flat, rollover (rounded), step (a ledge), concave (scooped). | **Baffle shape**, **Baffle height**, **Where the baffle begins**, **Baffle curve**, **Baffle hump** |
| **Floor** / **ramp** | The bottom of the inside, from the window's back edge to the throat. A ramp is a floor that slopes gradually away from the reed instead of dropping. | **Chamber floor**, **Chamber height**; the floor line in **Edit shape** (Inside chart) |
| **Side walls** | The inside walls on each side of the window, from the rails up to the roof: straight, leaning out (scooped) or leaning in. | **Chamber side walls** |
| **Chamber** | The open space between the baffle and the throat. Its cross-section can be round, square or horseshoe. | **Chamber shape**, **Chamber width**, **Widening after the throat**, **How far the full width runs**, **Chamber height** |
| **Throat** | The narrowest point inside, where the chamber meets the bore. | **Throat position**, **Throat width**, **Narrowing into the throat**, **Throat shape** |
| **Bore** | The round tube from the throat back to the socket. | **Bore (behind the cork)**, **Table angle to the neck** |
| **Socket** | The back of the bore, where the neck cork goes in. | **Neck cork diameter**, **Cork squeeze**, **How far the cork goes in**, **Lead-in at the opening** |
| **Inside air volume** | All the air from the neck's end to the tip, with the reed closed. Shown in the readouts. | the chamber, baffle and length settings together |

## Outside

| Part | What it is | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Beak** | The sloping top in front, from the tip back to the shoulder; it rests on the top teeth. | **Beak height at the tip**, **Beak curve**, **Beak top**, **Beak top width**, **Beak length** |
| **Shoulder** | Where the beak meets the body. | **Beak length**, **Shoulder smoothness**, **Shoulder sweep** |
| **Body** | The middle, at its widest and tallest; the ligature sits here. | **Body width**, **Body height**, **Body cross-section**, **Sides near the table** |
| **Shank** | The back end, around the socket. | **Shank outside diameter**; **Shank decoration** and **Text around the shank** (Personalize) |
| **Length** | From the neck end to the tip. | **Length** |

## Around it

| Part | What it is | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| **Reed** | Its heel lies on the table; its thin tip vibrates against the facing and tip rail. | **Table length** matches the table to it |
| **Ligature** | The band that holds the reed on the table. | **Ligature** (on the left) makes one to fit |
| **Cap** | The cover over the tip and reed. | **Cap** (on the left) makes one to fit |
