The facing is the part of the table that curves away from the reed, from the **break** (where
the rails leave the flat table) to the tip. This is its length, measured along the table.

With the mouthpiece on a glass plate, a very thin feeler gauge (0.0015") slid in from the tip stops
just short of the break, where the gap gets too small to measure. Longer = the curve starts further back.
